## Match overview

IFK Norrköping FK defeated Degerfors IF 1–0. The supplied passing dashboards show a clear difference in passing volume and completion: IFK recorded 553 passes with 445 successful (80%), while Degerfors recorded 309 passes with 215 successful (70%).

The figures indicate that IFK completed more passes and sustained a higher overall completion rate. Passing volume alone, however, does not establish territorial control, chance quality or the reason for the result. The maps and individual dashboards below provide the context for examining how each team distributed the ball.

## Key observations

- **IFK recorded the higher passing volume:** 553 attempts compared with Degerfors’ 309.
- **IFK also had the higher completion rate:** 80% against 70%.
- **Degerfors’ passing output was more limited:** 94 of 309 attempts were unsuccessful, compared with 108 unsuccessful passes from IFK’s 553 attempts.
- **Individual involvement varied within Degerfors:** Sebastian Ohlsson recorded 37 passes (28 successful, 76%), while Christos Gravius recorded 29 (22 successful, 76%) and 20 passes received.
- **Gyau combined a useful completion rate with a direct contribution:** 20 of 25 passes completed (80%) and one key pass.
- **The dashboards show different individual passing profiles:** Johan Mårtensson completed all 13 recorded passes, while Rasmus Örqvist completed 15 of 29 (52%). Diego De Jesus Campos Ballestero recorded 27 passes (15 successful, 56%) alongside four shots, two on target.
- **Pashang Abdulla’s dashboard records 4 successful passes from 12 attempts (33%).** Abdelkarim Mammar Chaouche’s dashboard covers a late appearance, from the 84th to the 99th minute, with 2 successful passes from 4 attempts.
