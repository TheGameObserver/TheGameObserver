## Match overview

Portugal defeated Sweden 3–2 in an international friendly on 20 May 2025 at Estádio da Luz, Lisbon. The supplied dashboards show Portugal with a higher share of possession and a substantially higher shot count.

## What the data reveals

- **Portugal had more of the ball:** 57% possession compared with Sweden’s 43%.
- **Portugal recorded greater passing volume:** 446 passes against Sweden’s 332.
- **Portugal’s displayed pass accuracy was higher:** 84% compared with 77%.
- **The shot-volume difference was pronounced:** Portugal recorded 29 shots; Sweden recorded 8.
- **Sweden recorded more interceptions:** 73 compared with Portugal’s 62, according to the supplied team comparison.

## Method and data note

This report summarises the figures displayed in the supplied raw-match-event dashboards. The dashboard’s goal breakdown includes an own-goal notation that is not sufficiently clear to reproduce as a separate team total; the final score is therefore reported as 3–2 without attempting to allocate the own goal. The underlying event file, event definitions, coordinate orientation and calculation methodology have not been independently audited for this publication. Treat the figures as dashboard-reported values rather than independently verified match statistics. Interpretations are limited to the displayed metrics; they do not establish causation.
