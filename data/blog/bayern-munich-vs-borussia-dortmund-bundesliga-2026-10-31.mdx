Bayern Munich had long periods of attacking pressure, but Borussia Dortmund found a way to remain competitive and leave the Allianz Arena with a 2–2 draw. Dortmund's resilience mattered, but so did their ability to threaten when opportunities appeared.

## Bayern pushed forward

Konrad Laimer gave Bayern the lead in the 38th minute. The home side continued to create attacking situations after the interval, but their advantage was not secure.

Dortmund equalised through Serhou Guirassy in the 60th minute. Bayern responded almost immediately through Dayot Upamecano, restoring their lead and putting Dortmund under pressure again.

## Dortmund stayed dangerous

Dortmund did not allow the match to become a one-way contest. They continued to search for forward opportunities and remained close enough to punish Bayern if the home side failed to protect their advantage.

Harry Kane missed a second-half penalty, which gave Dortmund another opportunity to stay in the match. In the 89th minute, substitute Fábio Silva scored the equaliser after a move involving substitute Georgios Karetsas.

## Key Tactical Findings

- Bayern created sustained attacking pressure but could not establish a decisive lead.
- Dortmund responded after both Bayern goals.
- Kane's missed penalty kept the visitors within reach.
- Dortmund's late attacking action secured the draw.

## TGO Analyst View

Dortmund's performance showed the value of remaining dangerous while defending under pressure. Bayern had enough attacking moments to win, but territorial control did not guarantee security. Dortmund stayed in the contest and took advantage when the opportunity arrived.

## Conclusion

The 2–2 draw was shaped by Bayern's attacking pressure and Dortmund's ability to respond. Dortmund did not need to control every phase; they needed to stay organised, remain within reach and make their late opportunity count.
