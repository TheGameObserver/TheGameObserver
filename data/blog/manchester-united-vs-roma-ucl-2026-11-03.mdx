Manchester United's 3–2 victory over Roma was a match of contrasting phases. Roma established a two-goal lead inside the opening 17 minutes, but United responded after the break and completed the comeback through Bruno Fernandes and Matheus Cunha.

## Roma made the early advantage count

Matías Soulé opened the scoring in the 11th minute, before Donyell Malen converted a penalty six minutes later. Roma's early success put United under pressure and forced the home side to chase the match.

United had to change the rhythm of the contest. Their response was more aggressive after half-time, with greater urgency in the attacking third.

## United's second-half response

Bruno Fernandes reduced the deficit from the penalty spot in the 58th minute. The goal gave United a route back into the match and increased the pressure on Roma's defence.

Cunha then scored twice late in the game to complete the comeback. The decisive goals came during a period when United were pushing forward and Roma were struggling to protect their lead.

The comeback should not be treated as proof that every first-half problem had been solved. Roma's early goals exposed weaknesses, while the final outcome was also shaped by individual execution and late defensive errors.

## Key Tactical Findings

- Roma punished United early and established a two-goal lead.
- United increased their attacking urgency after the interval.
- Fernandes' penalty changed the momentum.
- Cunha's late goals completed the turnaround.

## TGO Analyst View

United's comeback was driven by persistence, increased attacking pressure and decisive actions in the final third. The match also exposed a clear warning: a strong second half does not erase the defensive problems that allowed Roma to take control early.

## Conclusion

United's victory showed how quickly game state can change. Roma began with a clear advantage, but United stayed in the contest and made their late attacking pressure count. The comeback was impressive, but both teams will take important lessons from the way the match moved between its two halves.
