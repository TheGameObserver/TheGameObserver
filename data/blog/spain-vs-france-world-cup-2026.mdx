# Spain vs France Tactical Analysis

Spain dominated possession through their positional structure and excellent midfield rotations, limiting France's ability to progress through central areas.

## Match Overview

Spain controlled the tempo through patient build-up and positional superiority. France looked dangerous in transition but struggled to sustain pressure.

## First Half

Spain consistently created overloads in midfield through Pedri, the inverted full-backs, and intelligent rotations. France's first line of pressure was bypassed repeatedly, allowing Spain to dictate possession.

## Second Half

France improved after tactical substitutions, but Spain responded by controlling possession and protecting the central zones. Their rest defence prevented dangerous counterattacks.

## Tactical Observations

- Spain maintained numerical superiority in midfield.
- France relied heavily on direct transitions.
- Spain's coordinated high press forced several turnovers.
- Wide players occupied the half-spaces to create passing triangles.
- Full-backs supported progression by inverting into midfield.

## Key Players

Pedri dictated the tempo, Lamine Yamal stretched the defence with his positioning, while Mbappé remained France's primary attacking outlet in transition.

## Conclusion

Spain deserved the victory through superior positional play, intelligent pressing, and consistent control of possession. Their tactical discipline ultimately proved decisive in reaching the World Cup Final.
