# Spain vs France Tactical Analysis

Spain's 2-0 victory over France in the FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-final was built on defensive discipline, effective midfield play and decisive moments in attack. Mikel Oyarzabal converted a penalty in the 22nd minute, and Pedro Porro doubled Spain's lead in the 58th. Spain protected the advantage with a composed performance that restricted France's attacking threat.

## Match Overview

France entered the semi-final with a dangerous attacking line led by Kylian Mbappé, but Spain limited the space and time available to France's forwards. Spain combined possession with a compact defensive structure, preventing France from sustaining pressure for long periods.

The opening goal came after Lamine Yamal was fouled in the penalty area by Lucas Digne. Oyarzabal converted from the spot in the 22nd minute. Porro's second-half finish gave Spain a two-goal cushion and changed the demands of the match for both teams.

## First Half

Spain's approach balanced midfield circulation with forward movement. Rodri and Fabián Ruiz helped provide the platform, while Dani Olmo connected midfield possession to the attacking line. Rather than relying on possession alone, Spain looked to progress when opportunities opened and use its attacking players to threaten the spaces around France's defensive block.

The decisive moment arrived in the 22nd minute. Yamal's movement drew the foul that led to the penalty, and Oyarzabal converted it. The goal gave Spain control of the scoreline and forced France to take greater risks as the match developed.

## Second Half

Spain extended its lead in the 58th minute. Pedro Porro finished a move involving Dani Olmo, rewarding Spain's ability to combine through the right side and turn an attacking opening into a clear chance.

At 2-0, Spain could prioritise control and defensive security. Its compactness and work without the ball reduced the space France could exploit, while possession helped Spain manage the tempo. France continued to search for a response, but Spain preserved its clean sheet and the two-goal advantage.

## Tactical Observations

- Spain paired midfield circulation with purposeful forward progression rather than treating possession as an end in itself.
- Rodri, Fabián Ruiz and Dani Olmo provided a central platform for Spain's build-up and combinations.
- Lamine Yamal's movement was decisive in the sequence that produced the opening penalty.
- Spain's second goal came from a combination involving Olmo and Porro, with Porro finishing in the 58th minute.
- Once ahead by two goals, Spain managed the match through defensive compactness and controlled possession.
- Spain limited France's ability to turn its attacking talent into sustained pressure and finished with a clean sheet.

## Key Players

**Mikel Oyarzabal** converted the penalty that gave Spain the lead. **Pedro Porro** scored the second goal, while **Lamine Yamal** made the decisive run that led to the penalty. **Dani Olmo** contributed to Spain's attacking combinations, and **Kylian Mbappé** remained France's most prominent attacking reference, although Spain restricted France's overall threat.

## Conclusion

Spain's 2-0 win was not simply a story of possession. It reflected the team's ability to combine midfield control with defensive organisation and convert key attacking moments. Oyarzabal's penalty established the lead, Porro's goal strengthened it, and Spain's game management protected the result.

The victory sent Spain through to the **FIFA World Cup 2026 final**, where they went on to defeat Argentina 1-0 after extra time and become world champions.
